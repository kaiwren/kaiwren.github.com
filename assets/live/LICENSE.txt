Marble 01 by Rob Tuytel / Poly Haven
https://polyhaven.com/a/marble_01
License: CC0 1.0 Universal (public domain)
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

marble.webp: 512 x 512, RGB diffuse, alpha roughness (quantized to 4 bits).
Derived from an individual slab cropped from the 1K diffuse and roughness maps.
The shader arranges and rotates these stone faces into concentric circular courses.
The shader neutralises the warm source white balance for the pale outdoor plaza.
